## Product Purpose

한일특장 (Hanil Special Equipment) is a contract manufacturer of exterior sheet-metal parts: bus and truck outer panels, refrigerated truck body press parts, tractor hoods and fenders, implement and attachment parts. The site exists to get a drawing sent in for review (new part quotes, transfers of existing parts, early development reviews).

## Positioning

Large deep-drawn panels formed on presses from 1,500 t down to 150 t, welded by robots and skilled welders, finished and inspected on one site, so nothing leaves mid-process and delivery dates hold. Revenue grew from KRW 2.13 bn (2023) to 6.04 bn (2025), target 10 bn in 2026; about three years of OEM order backlog.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive with drawings and a part in mind; they compare suppliers on capacity (press tonnage, welding), process control, current customers and growth. Contact is by e-mail with drawings attached, or phone.
