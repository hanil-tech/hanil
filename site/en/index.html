Eight steps from steel to shipment

How cold-rolled steel becomes a finished part.

  1. 01

    Incoming steel

    Grade and thickness checked and logged.

  2. 02

    Shearing

    Cut to size, nested to keep offcuts low.

  3. 03

    Forming

    Pressed in up to four stages, by part.

  4. 04

    Welding

    Robots do the bulk; skilled welders finish the rest.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Beads and defects ground away for paint quality.

  6. 06

    Inspection

    Dimensions and finish checked; only passes move on.

  7. 07

    Painting

    Partner paint shops, to customer specification.

  8. 08

    Shipping

    Packed to quantity with a delivery statement.

Frequently asked questions

What parts do you make?

Visible outer sheet-metal parts: bus and truck outer panels, refrigerated body press parts, tractor hoods and fenders, front loader booms, mid-mount mowers and implement parts.

What material do you use?

Cold-rolled steel, checked for grade and thickness on arrival before forming.

Can you deliver painted parts?

Yes. Painting is done by partner paint shops to your colour and film specification.

Can you take over a part made elsewhere?

Yes, we review transfers of existing parts. Send us the drawing.

Do you export?

Our first export, small fender cover sets, went to Taiwan in 2025.
