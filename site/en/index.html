Eight steps from steel to shipment

How cold-rolled steel becomes a finished part.

  1. 01

    Incoming steel

    Grade and thickness checked and logged.

  2. 02

    Shearing

    Cut to size, nested to keep offcuts low.

  3. 03

    Forming

    Pressed in up to four stages, by part.

  4. 04

    Welding

    Robots do the bulk; skilled welders finish the rest.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Beads and defects ground away for paint quality.

  6. 06

    Inspection

    Dimensions and finish checked; only passes move on.

  7. 07

    Painting

    Partner paint shops, to customer specification.

  8. 08

    Shipping

    Packed to quantity with a delivery statement.
